## Mixed playlists

Build a stream playlist with songs from Spotify and YouTube together. Open **Listening → Your playlists**, create a playlist, then add songs or append playlists from either service.

Choose **Use for stream** to keep it playing when chat is quiet. Requests go next after the current song, followed by your personal queue and then the stream playlist. You can rename, reorder and edit saved playlists without interrupting the song that's playing.

## Other changes

- Saved playlists are included in backups and restored when you reopen Encore.
- Failed imports leave your existing songs in place; duplicate songs from the same service are skipped.
- Reworked the README and project guides, and removed the development diary.

Existing settings and overlay URLs continue to work.
