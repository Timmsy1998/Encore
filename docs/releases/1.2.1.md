## Shuffle stream playlists

Choose **Shuffle for stream** in **Listening → Your playlists** to play a saved playlist in a randomised order. Choose **Use for stream** to return to its saved order.

The current song finishes first. Requests still take priority, followed by your personal queue and then the stream playlist. Shuffling leaves the saved playlist unchanged, and the shuffled rotation is restored when you reopen Encore with playback paused.

Automated checks cover shuffle ordering, request priority, persistence and the playlist controls at desktop display sizes. Real-account playback and manual Windows installation, listening and update checks were not repeated for this patch.
