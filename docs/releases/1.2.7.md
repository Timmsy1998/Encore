## Alternative versions and playback history

Find another version from History, the current song on Listening, or a restricted-video error. Encore searches Spotify and YouTube with platform labels and excludes the original recording. Choose a result to add it to personal listening; nothing is added automatically.

History now records the latest 1,000 completed and skipped plays, including timestamps, provider, requester and skip reasons: manual skips, viewer votes, bans and YouTube embedding restrictions. Filter completed/skipped plays, search the history or queue a song again. Older accepted requests are preserved internally without being presented as completed plays. History renders 50 rows initially to keep resource use bounded.

YouTube continues playing and rotating tracks while Encore is minimised. Restricted videos reporting errors 101 or 150 are skipped automatically instead of pausing the queue. Discord Rich Presence survives song changes and retries transient update failures.
